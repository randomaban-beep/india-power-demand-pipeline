Methodology & Sources

Data Ingestion, Cleaning Rules & Econometric Limits

Transparent accounting of all sources, cleaning decisions, econometric estimators, and observational limitations governing the pipeline findings.

Primary Data Sources

This analytics pipeline ingests and harmonizes three independent data streams:

  • Grid Controller of India (NLDC): Daily PSP operational reports published under Daily Reports. 769 reports ingested covering 2021 to 2026.
  • Mendeley Data (DOI: 10.17632/y58jknpgs8.2): Hourly PMU and SCADA telemetry spanning Sept 2021 to Jun 2025 (23,184 rows across 966 distinct days).
  • Open-Meteo Historical Archive: Hourly ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis weather data (temperature, humidity, wind) across Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, and Ahmedabad.

Data Cleaning & Integrity Rules

Real-world SCADA telemetry contains dropouts, sensor freezes, and unfreeze spikes that produce false extremes if not properly handled:

  • Multi-Hour Dips: Dips of 1 to 4 hours collapsing below surrounding baseline and rebounding are detected and set to NULL (177 hours; never silently interpolated).
  • Stuck Sensor Runs: Flatlined runs of ≥ 3 hours of identical non-zero values (such as the 64,301 MW solar flatline on 2025-03-07) are set to NULL.
  • Nighttime Solar Leaks: Solar generation exceeding 500 MW between 20:00 and 05:00 IST is set to NULL (329 hours).
  • Unfreeze Edge Protection: The transition hour immediately following any sensor freeze or dropout is nullified, eliminating artificial 30+ GW unfreeze jumps.

Econometric Regression Model

Temperature sensitivity is modeled using an OLS regression with a hinge term on daily peak demand met:

  • Hinge Function: CDD = max(T - 25.0°C, 0), evaluating marginal cooling demand above a 25.0°C cooling base.
  • Categorical Controls: Day-of-week, calendar month, and multi-year trend dummies isolate macroeconomic growth and regular calendar patterns (controls R² = 0.793).
  • Robust Covariance: White's Heteroskedasticity-Consistent (HC1) covariance matrix prevents variance bias under extreme weather swings.
  • Sensitivity Range: Candidate thresholds from 22.0°C to 28.0°C span +5,977 to +10,064 MW/°C across the same 743 daily observations.

Limitations & Assumptions

Key observational caveats to consider when interpreting findings:

  • Archive Recording Gaps: The Mendeley dataset contains 2 documented multi-month logging campaign gaps (2022-10 to 2023-03 and 2023-05 to 2023-12).
  • Reanalysis Weather: Open-Meteo atmospheric variables represent ECMWF ERA5 numerical model reanalysis simulations rather than physical surface weather station sensors.
  • Renewable Denominator: In hourly SCADA telemetry, renewable share is reported as a percentage of electricity demand (averaging 11.1%), whereas Grid-India daily reports compute share of gross generation.
